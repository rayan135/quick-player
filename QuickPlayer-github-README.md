# Quick Player (QP)

A free Windows app for watching your own Emby and Jellyfin media servers, shared with friends and family. Its app name on Trakt is **QP**.

## What it does

- Plays shows, movies and anime from the Emby and Jellyfin servers you add, with subtitles, audio choices and skip intro.
- Keeps your watch history, Continue Watching, likes and watchlist on your own PC.
- Optionally connects to **Trakt** (TV and movies) and **AniList** (anime). Each person signs in with their own account, and what they watch in the app is added to their history there.
- Shows Trakt recommendations, trending titles and a weekly calendar of what's airing, and marks which of them are already on your servers.

## Trakt notice

**QP uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.**

The same notice is shown inside the app, in Settings → Trackers (under the Trakt and AniList sign-in) and in Settings → General → About.

## Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

### The short version

Quick Player runs on your PC. It has no server of its own, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer never receives your data. The app only talks to the media servers you add and, if you sign in, to Trakt and AniList.

### What stays on your PC

Your server sign-ins, watch history, Continue Watching, likes, watchlist, followed and dropped shows, and settings are saved in the `Quick Player` folder in your Windows user folder. They leave your PC only if you export them to a file yourself.

### Your media servers

Quick Player connects to the Emby or Jellyfin servers you add. Like any Emby or Jellyfin app, the server you play from sees what you play and where you stop.

### Trakt (only if you sign in)

- **Sign-in:** you enter a short code on trakt.tv; the app never sees your Trakt password. The access it receives is stored encrypted for your Windows user, on your PC only.
- **What QP sends to Trakt:** episodes and movies you finish watching (with the time), where you stopped (if that option is on), what you mark as watched, likes (as Trakt favorites), watchlist changes, titles you mark "Not interested", and your past history only when you press "Send my whole history".
- **What QP reads from Trakt:** your watch history (only when you press "Pull from Trakt"), your recommendations, trending titles, the TV calendar, and whether a title is already in your history, favorites or watchlist.
- Nothing from Trakt is sold, shared or sent anywhere else.

### How to disconnect from Trakt

- In the app: Settings → Trackers → Sign out. This deletes QP's Trakt access from your PC.
- On trakt.tv: open your account settings, go to Connected Apps, and revoke QP. QP can then no longer read or change your Trakt data.

### AniList (only if you sign in)

The same idea for anime: your progress on the anime you watch, favourites, your Planning list and titles you mark as completed are sent to your AniList account. Your list, recommendations, trending and the airing schedule are read from it. To disconnect: Settings → Trackers → Sign out, or revoke the app in your AniList settings.

### Removing everything

Deleting the `Quick Player` folder in your Windows user folder removes everything the app kept on your PC.
